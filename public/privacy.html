គោលការណ៍ឯកជនភាព (Privacy Policy)

Effective Date: November 2026  |  App Name: AI Lesson Plan Studio

1. Overview (សេចក្ដីសង្ខេប)

AI Lesson Plan Studio is a web-based educational tool designed to help Cambodian educators create MoEYS Standard and Flipped Classroom lesson plans, assessments, and teaching presentations. We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device (ទិន្នន័យរក្សាទុកក្នុងឧបករណ៍ផ្ទាល់ខ្លួន)

  • Your lesson plans, drafts, custom templates, teacher profile information, and Gemini API key are stored locally in your own browser (localStorage).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your lesson content or API keys with any third-party marketers or advertisers.

3. Google Slides API & Google User Data (ការប្រើប្រាស់ Google Slides API)

When you choose to sign in with Google to export a lesson presentation to docs.google.com/presentation, AI Lesson Plan Studio requests only the minimal non-sensitive per-file scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (access limited strictly to presentations created by this app).

  • Purpose: Strictly to create a new Google Slides presentation containing the lesson slides and speaker notes that you explicitly requested.
  • No Reading or Sharing of Existing Files: The app does not read, scan, copy, or delete any of your existing Google Drive or Google Slides files.
  • No Server Storage of OAuth Tokens: Your Google OAuth access token is held temporarily in browser memory only during your active session and is never stored on our servers. You can sign out or revoke access at any time via your Google Account security settings.
  • Google API Limited Use Disclosure: AI Lesson Plan Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-Party AI Processing (ការភ្ជាប់ AI API)

When you generate lesson plans, quizzes, or quality audits, the lesson text you enter is sent directly to the AI provider whose API key you configured (such as Google Gemini API) solely to generate your requested educational content.

5. Contact Information (ទំនាក់ទំនង)

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request support, please contact the developer at Kampong Cham Teacher Education College via the official website: https://lessonplan-kh.vercel.app.
